BluePad — icon & splash concepts

Round 2 — your sketch: the B of BluePad as the controller outline — d-pad cross in one bowl, the four face-button dots in the other, and the Bluetooth rune tilted 45° in the middle section — drafted in two takes: D · solid (filled B, details knocked out) and E · line (bold line-art). Round 1's A–C didn't land; they stay switchable for reference. Direction unchanged: flat, dark ground, blue accent, device-agnostic; palette from the UI prototype: ground #0b1738, accent #5b8dff. Flip with the bar below or ←/→. Each concept is shown across the full appinfo set: icon.png 130×130, icon_large.png 320×320, small-size legibility, launcher context, splash.png, and iconColor.

A · Solid glyph

The literal reading of the direction: a solid gamepad silhouette in relief navy on the brand ground, the Bluetooth rune overlaid down the middle in full accent. D-pad and face buttons are knocked out to sell "gamepad" without naming any device. Safest at launcher size — the silhouette carries at 48 px, the rune still resolves.
