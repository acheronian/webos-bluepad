BluePad

0.1.0 · com.acheronian.bluepad
Bluetooth gamepad manager for webOS 4.x. Pairs and connects pads over the app-owned HID session (raw L2CAP + uhid) with the stock path as fallback, and delivers input to streaming apps such as punktfunk and Moonlight.
Installed via the Homebrew Channel · MIT / Apache-2.0 · based on the punktfunk client-webos architecture.
